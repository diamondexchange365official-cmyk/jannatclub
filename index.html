What is Jannat Club?+

Jannat Club brings lottery, arcade, mini games, and casino-style categories into one mobile interface. This website is a visual guide to the screens and features shown in the supplied app images.

How do I create an account?+

The registration screen asks for a phone number, a password, password confirmation, and an invite code where applicable. Follow the registration walkthrough to understand the form.

How do I download the Android app?+

See the download and installation guide. Select Download app to save full.apk to your device, then follow the installation steps.

Which payment methods appear in the app?+

The supplied deposit screen shows UPI QR options, USDT, and ARPay. These are screenshots, not payment services on this website. Available methods and conditions must be checked in the actual app.

How do withdrawals work?+

The withdrawal screen provides bank-card, UPI, and USDT options and asks for beneficiary details. The withdrawal guide explains the fields. Current limits and processing conditions are set by the platform.

Are bonuses or winnings guaranteed?+

No. Game outcomes are uncertain and money can be lost. Any offer is subject to its current eligibility requirements and terms. Treat games as entertainment and set a limit before playing.

Can I register or make payments on this website?+

No. The registration button opens the Jannat Club signup page. Login and support links open the Telegram channel. It does not create accounts, accept deposits, or process withdrawals.
